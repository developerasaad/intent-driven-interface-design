Formwork designs and makes furniture for the rooms people actually live in. The collection is short on purpose: fewer pieces, considered for longer.

01 — About

A short list of pieces, made with care.

Formwork is a furniture practice working across seating, tables, storage and lighting. The collection is deliberately short: a few pieces, each developed through full-size prototypes and a narrow set of materials.

We design for rooms that get used — chairs that can be pulled up to a desk, tables that take a spill, storage that hides the mess. Pieces are made in small runs, so a finish can be adjusted and a detail can change between batches.

A room should be able to change without the furniture changing with it.

02 — Approach

How a piece is developed

01

Draw

Each piece begins with a full-size drawing and a set of constraints: seat height, door swing, the width of a hallway.

02

Prototype

We build in the workshop before committing to a finish. Proportions are adjusted at full scale, not on a screen.

03

Finish

Surfaces are oiled, waxed or powder-coated. The aim is a finish that can be repaired, not one that has to be replaced.

04 — Questions

Before you ask.

Can I see a finish in person?

Yes. Finish samples are kept at the studio, and a small set can be posted if you cannot visit. The oak, ash, wool and steel samples show how each surface looks in daylight.

How long does an order take?

Lead times vary by piece and finish. The studio confirms timing in writing before an order is placed, so there are no assumptions on either side.

Do you deliver outside Portland?

Delivery is quoted per address. For larger pieces, the studio arranges a carrier and confirms access details before the piece is released.

Do you work with trade clients?

Yes. The studio works with interior designers, architects and commercial clients on single pieces and small runs. Start with a note and include the project location and timeline.
